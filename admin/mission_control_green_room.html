Mission Sequence

1. Mission target named.
2. Countdown begins.
3. GENE performs preflight and reports readiness.
4. Human decides LAUNCH or HOLD.
5. Human ignites kiln.
6. GENE confirms ignition from evidence.
7. Firing states and narration unfold through the mission.
8. Recovery and debrief close the record.
