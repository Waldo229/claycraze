Where the work takes form

Making starts with a place to work, and what makes a good workspace differs from person to person. I’ve worked in schools, community centers, private studios, and cooperative workshops. Each has its own atmosphere. Even a modest home studio feels different.

Working at home gives me the most freedom. It also means I’m responsible for everything from cleaning and mixing to keeping the studio organized and firing the kilns.
