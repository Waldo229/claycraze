Studio

Step one in the creative process is to have a suitable workspace. This means different things to different people.

I have experimented with using schools, community centers, private studios, and co-operative workshops. All offer different atmospheres, and none are quite like a home studio, no matter how modest.

The least restrictive environment will, in my opinion, be a home studio. The trade-off for freedom, however, is that you become responsible for all the cleaning, mixing, organization, and firing.
