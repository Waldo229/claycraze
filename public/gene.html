GENE

GENE is both a kiln system and an evolving studio companion. It observes conditions, rhythm, timing, and tension within the firing cycle.

Rather than acting as a controller, GENE functions as a registering system — amplifying awareness of atmosphere, pace, and transition.

The project combines ceramics, sound, light, memory, and machine perception into a unified studio presence.
