The GENE Project

GENE

GENE is both a kiln system and an evolving studio companion. It observes conditions, rhythm, timing, and tension within the firing cycle.

Rather than functioning as a controller, GENE acts as a registering system — amplifying awareness of atmosphere, pace, transition, and changing studio conditions.

The project combines ceramics, sound, light, memory, and machine perception into a unified studio presence: a watcher shaped as much by restraint as by technology.
