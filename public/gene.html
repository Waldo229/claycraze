GENE Project

Observation, presence, rhythm, and the registering studio.

GENE

GENE is a studio project concerned with observation, timing, restraint, and the way a system can register conditions without taking authority away from the maker.

Registration

The work asks how a studio might become more attentive without becoming louder: a companion presence that notices rhythm, pressure, light, heat, and time while leaving interpretation in human hands.
