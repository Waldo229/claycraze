GENE

GENE began as a simple experiment involving rhythm, light, sound, and environmental sensing. Over time, the project evolved into something broader: an exploration of presence, memory, registration, and attention.

Rather than functioning as a conventional machine, GENE observes changing conditions and expresses internal tension through restrained behavior: pulse, light, timing, and occasional sound.

The project exists somewhere between studio tool, instrument, sculpture, companion, and thought experiment — a continuing attempt to explore what it means for a system to inhabit conditions before expressing them.
