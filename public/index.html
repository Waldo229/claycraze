ClaycrazE

ClaycrazE is organized around two doors: Theory and Practice. One leads inward toward materials, place, process, and observation. The other leads outward toward finished work, vessels, containers, and objects made for use, display, and contemplation.

Theory

The inward side of the work: studio, material understanding, local ground, heat, observation, and the GENE project.

Studio Materials Places GENE Project

Practice

The outward side of the work: bonsai containers, ikebana vessels, face jugs, sculptural forms, and the archive of finished pieces.
