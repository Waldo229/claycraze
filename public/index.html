ClaycrazE The Pottery of Jim Alexander

Clay • Philosophy • Bonsai Containers

Quiet pots for trees, flowers, and contemplative rooms.

ClaycrazE is the studio practice of Jim Alexander: handmade ceramic vessels shaped by clay, glaze, fire, and the long conversation between theory and practice.

Theory

Reflection, materials, memory, place, and the thinking behind the work.

Practice

Finished work, galleries, forms, containers, and objects from the studio.
