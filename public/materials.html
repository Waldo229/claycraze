The physical vocabulary of the studio.

Materials

Pottery begins with materials: clay bodies, glaze ingredients, oxides, ash, stone, and the geological history carried inside them.

Different materials behave differently under heat. Some encourage movement and gloss, while others produce quiet, dry, restrained surfaces. Understanding these tendencies becomes part chemistry, part observation, and part accumulated studio memory.

Much of my interest centers around historical materials, local geology, atmospheric firing, and the subtle behavior that emerges when materials interact slowly through time and heat.
