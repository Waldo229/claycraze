Artist’s Statement

Jim Alexander

I create objects that endure, sometimes quietly, like weathered sentinels marking time and place. I work in clay and paint, skirting the boundaries between art and craft. A tree can be held within the bounds of its pot and still reach out into its world. A portrait can hold the feeling of a moment long enough for someone else to stand witness.

Like GENE, the kiln watcher I made for my studio, I am a sentinel. I can record only what I see from where I stand. For me, fidelity means staying true to what I’ve seen and leaving room for what I may have missed. I depend on others, made of the same materials and seeing from different vantage points, to help me discern my own truth.

This is my world, and you are welcome to visit. What you see here will depend, in part, on where you stand. I am forever a student, and this is my journey.
