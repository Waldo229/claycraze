The GENE Project

Studio instrument

A Kiln Spirit for the Twenty-First Century

GENE — Guardian of Emergent New Experiences

GENE is home to the kiln watcher. The project explores what happens when the traditional kiln spirit becomes articulate.

Over time, GENE learns to pay attention. The spirit is no longer a mute sentinel, but an oracle, articulating in its own language patterns, changes, and departures from expectation.

GENE amplifies the idea that the whole is more than the sum of its parts, and that simplicity, registration, and attunement are virtues.

GENE never intervenes. But when asked, it may reveal.
