Theory / Kiln

Heat, time, transformation, and reveal.

The kiln is the transforming field of the studio: a place where clay, glaze, atmosphere, temperature, patience, and surrender meet.

Kiln

The kiln changes clay and glaze into something no longer merely assembled by hand, but altered by heat, atmosphere, time, and temperature.

Electric and gas firing each offer a different kind of attention. The kiln asks for planning, patience, and restraint; the final surface is the result of both intention and surrender.
