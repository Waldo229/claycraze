Privacy

Last updated September 21, 2026.

ClaycrazE is a small pottery-studio website. It does not provide visitor accounts or online checkout, and ClaycrazE does not use advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Information you choose to send

If you contact ClaycrazE by email, your message may include your name, email address, and anything else you choose to share. That information is used to answer you, discuss the work, or arrange a local purchase, pickup, event, or studio matter. It is kept only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and related business records.

Ordinary technical records

The companies that host and protect this website may create ordinary server and security logs. Those records can include an Internet address, browser or device information, requested pages, and the date and time of a request. ClaycrazE does not use those records to build advertising profiles.

Outside services

ClaycrazE links to social-media pages and other outside websites, and some pages may display externally hosted media. Those services operate under their own privacy practices once you visit or use them.

Sharing and sale of information

ClaycrazE does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be handled by service providers that operate the website or email service, or disclosed when reasonably required by law, security, or safety.

Your choice

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information you have sent directly to ClaycrazE, subject to records that must reasonably be retained. Write to jim@claycraze.com.

Children

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13.
