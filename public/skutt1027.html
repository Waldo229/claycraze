Theory / Kiln

Richie

Heat, timing, atmosphere, and restraint.

Richie is the studio’s Skutt 1027 electric kiln. Programmable control allows careful management of firing schedules, cooling cycles, glaze development, and the passage of heat through time.

Over repeated firings, a kiln becomes more than equipment. Its habits become familiar, its pace becomes legible, and its history enters the working memory and cadence of the studio.
