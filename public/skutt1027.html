Theory / Kiln

Heat, timing, atmosphere, and restraint.

Firing is not merely temperature control. It is rhythm, pacing, observation, and the slow negotiation between material and time.

Skutt 1027

The Skutt 1027 forms the primary electric kiln system within the ClaycrazE studio.

Reliable programmable control allows careful management of firing schedules, cooling cycles, glaze development, and atmospheric rhythm.

Over time the kiln becomes more than equipment — it becomes part of the working memory and cadence of the studio itself.
