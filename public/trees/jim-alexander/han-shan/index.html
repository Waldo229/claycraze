Han Shan

Juniper

Status: nfs

Credit: Collection of Jim Alexander

I acquired this tree in 2018 and in 2023 took it to a Jenifer Price workshop sponsored by the Virginia Bonsai Society. Jenifer observed that the tree was weak and in desperate need of re-potting. She advised that I should wait at least two years, maybe three, before thinking about styling. It was her opinion that the tree is roughly 80 year old. It appears that the tree has become more healthy and vigorous and I look forward to perhaps returning with it when she conducts another session for the club.
