Processes

Forming, handling, revision, and studio method.

Working Method

My process favors directness, adaptation, and close attention to material behavior. I am interested in techniques that remain legible in the finished work rather than methods that erase the hand entirely.

Making

The work develops through repeated decisions: forming, drying, trimming, surface treatment, firing, and revision. Each step leaves evidence, and that evidence is part of the final character of the piece.

Materials

The clay, glaze, and slip systems that shape the work’s surface.

Kiln

The transforming field of heat and time.

Studio

The habits and conditions that support the making.
