  • Peninsula Council of Garden Clubs

    Bonsai Gardening

    On September 11, 2026, Jim Alexander of ClaycrazE Studio and Chris Schmuck of Ironwood Bonsai will present a program on growing suitable material for bonsai, developing trees, and choosing containers.

    The program begins with the garden itself: how ordinary growing space can become the source of future bonsai material. From there, the discussion moves naturally through development, styling, containers, local clubs, resources, and possible workshop opportunities.

    Scheduled for September 11, 2026. Additional attendance details will be added when confirmed.
