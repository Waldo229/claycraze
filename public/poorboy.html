Theory / Experiment

Improvisation, adaptation, and persistence.

Some of the strongest lessons in ceramics emerge not from abundance, but from limitation, necessity, weather, repair, and making do with what is available.

The Poor Boy

Before larger kilns and dedicated systems, there were simpler solutions assembled through necessity.

The Poor Boy represents experimentation, constraint, learning, adaptation, and persistence — the practical side of developing a studio practice.

Improvised systems often teach lessons that polished systems conceal. Through limitation, the studio learns attention, patience, repair, and resilience.
