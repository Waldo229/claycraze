Theory / Places

Environments shape attention.

Landscapes, coastlines, gardens, rivers, workshops, and forgotten corners become part of the visual and emotional vocabulary surrounding the work.

Places

Creative work does not emerge in isolation. It is shaped by geography, climate, architecture, travel, memory, and the atmosphere of particular places.

Tidewater Virginia, the Great Dismal Swamp, old industrial areas, coastal geology, gardens, museums, workshops, and historical ceramics all contribute to the visual language surrounding the work.

Some places influence through beauty, others through silence, weather, erosion, age, or the traces left behind by people. Over time these environments become part of the studio vocabulary itself.
