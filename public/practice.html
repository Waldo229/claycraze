Practice

Practice is the outward side of the work: bonsai containers, ikebana vessels, face jugs, sculpture, and finished forms.

These pages gather together the made work itself: objects shaped through clay, glaze, firing, revision, accident, and long repetition.

Shih-te stands here as guide toward making, labor, touch, utility, and the life of the hand.

Bonsai

High-fired bonsai containers for trees, landscape, and display.

Ikebana

Vessels for floral arrangement, line, balance, and negative space.

Face Jugs

Grotesques, spirit vessels, humor, expression, and Southern face jug traditions.

Sculpture

Figurative and experimental forms outside purely utilitarian pottery.
