Hold a pot for an event

ClaycrazE pottery may be held for viewing and pickup at an upcoming local event.

  • Holds are first come, first served.
  • Holds are limited to three pieces per customer.
  • There is no obligation to purchase and no penalty for declining after seeing the work.
  • Payment is made at pickup. Cash, check, and Square credit-card payments are accepted.
  • Holds expire at the end of the event.
  • If you cannot attend, please call or text to request another arrangement.
  • Shipping is not currently offered.
